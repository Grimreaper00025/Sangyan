# 09 — Physical shares: a coherent extension with harder boundaries

## Why it fits

A physical certificate is an older representation of a security and its recorded holders. The same evidence model can connect it to later issuer events and demat credits. It adds certificate/folio identifiers, more uncertain documents and institutional verification requirements.

The compelling user question is: **“What does this old paper establish, what happened to the security, and what evidence is missing before I can claim it?”**

## Separate four routes

| Situation | Research route | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|
| Living registered holder with valid certificate | Ordinary dematerialisation / relevant service request | Treating every certificate as a lost-asset recovery case |
| Securities purchased earlier but transfer never registered | Check special transfer-window eligibility | Applying the window to any old holding |
| Registered holder deceased | Transmission route, with nominee/survivor/heir distinctions | Treating a signed historical transfer deed as proof of inheritance |
| Securities already transferred to IEPF | Entitlement verification and IEPF claim process | Assuming an ordinary DP demat request alone can recover them |

Overlapping complications—lost certificate, disputed title, name mismatch, old pledge, multiple deaths—require additional analysis. A classification should explain its evidence and remain revisable.

## Special transfer window: verified narrow opportunity

SEBI's 30 January 2026 circular covers eligible physical securities sold/purchased before 1 April 2019, with a window from **5 February 2026 to 4 February 2027**. Original certificates and qualifying historical transfer evidence matter. IEPF and disputed cases are excluded. The circular specifies processing within 70 days after documents are complete and a one-year lock-in after registration. It also addresses some untraceable/non-cooperating transferor situations; that does not waive every evidence requirement. [S023](sources.md#s023)

This deadline is not a universal deadline for all physical shareholders to dematerialise.

## Direct credit has already replaced an older extra step

SEBI's January 2026 investor-service reform, effective **2 April 2026**, removes the LOC requirement for the covered service requests in favour of direct demat credit after due diligence. It contains transitional treatment for earlier LOCs. [S024](sources.md#s024)

Do not build a workflow whose claimed innovation is eliminating a paper step the regulator has already removed. Use the current consolidated source and later amendments for implementation; Chapter 12 covers the later transmission framework.

## What software could resolve independently

- Extract certificate fields and identify which ones are illegible or contradictory.
- Find a supported historical issuer identity and a curated corporate-action chain.
- Calculate expected quantities conditional on confirmed eligibility and prior events.
- Identify the institutional destination and a versioned documentary route.
- Assemble an evidence index, correspondence chronology and unresolved questions.
- Reconcile eventual credit with the expected transformation and preserve original history.

## What it cannot establish from a photo alone

Current validity, cancellation/replacement status, pledge status, final beneficial entitlement, undisclosed transfers, disputed succession, or guaranteed recoverable market value. A photo may be enough to start research but not enough to establish title.

## Existing assistance

Zerodha's public dematerialisation initiative and recovery firms already offer practical help. [S037](sources.md#s037), [S038](sources.md#s038), [S039](sources.md#s039), [S040](sources.md#s040)

The potential software contribution is less repetitive evidence work and better continuity—not pretending no help exists. Measure document corrections and verified credits against an existing assistance route.

## Valuation discipline

Keep separate: certificate denomination, reconstructed quantity, confirmed claim quantity, current quoted value, restrictions and actual cash received. Never multiply an old face value or an unverified reconstructed quantity by a live price and label the result “recovered.”

## Recommended depth

Research physical shares as an adjacent module. For a four-day demonstration, include one thoroughly documented issuer chain or one uncontested transmission case. Nationwide certificate identification plus every succession scenario would be a separate programme of work.
