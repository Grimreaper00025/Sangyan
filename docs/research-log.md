# Research method, corrections and exclusions

**Cut-off:** 1 October 2026, India context. **Scope:** demat acquisition history expanded at the user's request into Track B inheritance/transmission and physical-share evidence.

## Method

Searched regulator/depository/exchange and tax sources, issuer filings, provider help centres, official audit/reform publications, public investor forums, and selected public financial commentary. Followed legal-page links to underlying circulars where accessible. Read the user-supplied six-page problem statement locally and inspected its Track B page visually.

Source quality hierarchy: enacted rule/judgment or issuer filing for legal/event facts; official operational documentation for stated workflows; vendor documentation for capabilities; public posts for human leads; commentary/video material for discovery only.

Each source in the catalogue records its class, access status, locator, use and caveat. Exact figures appear with dates and denominators. The arithmetic examples are independently generated and explicitly synthetic.

## Material corrections made during research

- The actual PDF specifies a four-day sprint; the website's seven-day wording is superseded for this dossier.
- The initial acquisition-only direction was expanded into inheritance at the user's explicit request. This is an evidence system, not merely a nominee tracker.
- MProfit has substantial corporate-action, transfer and reconciliation coverage. “Nobody solves this” was rejected.
- ITC Hotels allocation is 86.49% / 13.51% in the inspected revised issuer guidance. An unverified 96.04% / 3.96% lead was rejected.
- Old NSDL/SEBI transmission thresholds surfaced in search. The July 2026 final circular governs the current research matrix; the March consultation is historical context.
- LOC/direct-credit and nominee-to-heir reporting reforms already exist. They cannot be pitched as absent infrastructure.
- A previous-owner-cost FMV fallback exists in the tax statute. “Missing old cost can never be addressed legally” is too broad.
- Current web-page timestamps do not make old underlying tables current. CDSL's dated August PDF was used instead of an older table embedded in its statistics page.
- Historical IEPF delay data were balanced against more recent reform evidence and Portal 2.0 announcements.
- SEBI's September 2026 TechSprint results were verified against the underlying two-page press release. Its participation figures differ from company publicity; the regulator's figures are used. Winner descriptions are separated from conclusions about why they won.
- An issuer page contained both updated transmission guidance and older service-process passages. Source authority alone does not establish that every paragraph is current.

## Access and evidence limitations

No paid-product trial, authenticated broker session, private claimant documents, interviews, RTA confirmation or live account integration was performed. Some sources were accessible only through indexed extracts. EAC-PM's PDF fetch failed; its figures are labelled accordingly. Some SEBI PDFs required direct text extraction in earlier research. No browsing access limitation was bypassed.

Public posts are not independently authenticated. Indexed YouTube/transcript material was not treated as having watched the entire video. The Think School and Nikhil Kamath material does not establish a new operational failure beyond the primary sources.

No exhaustive review of every amendment, state succession/stamp law or issuer history was completed. This dossier identifies current key changes and the remaining legal review needed; it is not a certified legal-rule database.

## Rejected or deferred claims

- Total IEPF balance equals losses attributable to missing acquisition history.
- Demat accounts equal unique affected retail investors.
- Every blank cost is filed as zero; every corrected cost reduces tax.
- Every nominee is the final beneficial heir.
- Every no-nominee case requires a court certificate.
- A photograph proves current entitlement or exact recoverable value.
- A published consultation or announced portal is already operative.
- A deadline breach automatically creates a specified cash compensation entitlement.
- A regulator's unrelated hackathon winners prove this jury's preferences.
- Foreign-market cost-basis rules apply to India: irrelevant Pakistan NCCPL search results were excluded.
