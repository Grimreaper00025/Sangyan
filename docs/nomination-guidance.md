# Virasat nomination guidance — source notes

**Verified 2 October 2026.** The app offers planning steps, not an institutional form, automated registration or legal entitlement determination.

| Account class | Primary source and locator | Narrow app use |
|---|---|---|
| Demat and MF folios | [SEBI 29 May 2026 circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2026/1780397706130.pdf), §§6, 9.3, 10.1, 13; Annexure A | Official online/offline nomination channels; request acknowledgement; check a statement or confirmation of registration. Effective 1 September 2026. |
| HDFC Bank deposit accounts only | [HDFC Bank NetBanking FAQ](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/need-help/net-banking-faqs), “Where can I view my Nominee and Account Holder details?” and “Can I add or change nominee in my account online?” | Accounts → select account → Account Details → nominee/account-holder details. Sole holders may add/change there; bank recommends joint holders visit a branch with the other holders. Verified 2 October 2026. |
| Bank deposits | [Banking Companies (Nomination) Rules, 2025](https://thc.nic.in/Central%20Governmental%20Rules/Banking%20Companies%20(Nomination)%20Rules,%202025.pdf), rules 1(2), 2(2), 2(12)–(13), form acknowledgement section | Ask the bank for its current nomination form or available e-nomination. Distinguish receipt from recorded registration. Effective 1 November 2025. |
| MF service channel and demat units | [AMFI Investor Corner — Nomination](https://www.amfiindia.com/investor/become-mf-distributor?zoneName=nomination), “How to Nominate?” and “For Units held in Electronic (Demat) Mode” | Contact the fund/registrar; units in demat use that demat nomination route. The unusual URL was opened and its actual investor nomination content inspected. |

The newer SEBI circular supersedes earlier securities nomination circulars. AMFI's page/form may contain older operational details, so it is used only for the service-channel distinction, not a universal current signature/document checklist. Bank rules are a separate authority.

Virasat does not display one-nominee limits, automatic freeze deadlines, registration guarantees or a universal inheritance rule. Joint/minor/other special cases and actual forms are confirmed with the institution. “Not added” can reflect a deliberate opt-out; the account holder chooses the next action.

The institution's acknowledgement of receipt and the actual registration record are different observations. The tracker requires the user to say a reviewed record shows registration before using “Checked in a record.” No authenticity check is performed.

The HDFC hint is matched locally only for bank accounts whose institution label is `hdfc` or `hdfcbank` after removing whitespace and case differences. It is not applied to HDFC Securities, HDFC Mutual Fund, other banks or other account types. Typed data is not sent for lookup. The app does not promise that every statement displays nominee details or that an application receipt confirms registration.
