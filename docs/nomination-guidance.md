# Virasat nomination guidance — source notes

**Reviewed 3 October 2026.** The app offers planning steps, not an institutional form, automated registration or legal entitlement determination.

| Account class | Primary source and locator | Narrow app use |
|---|---|---|
| Demat and MF folios | [SEBI 29 May 2026 circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2026/1780397706130.pdf), §§6, 9.3, 10.1, 13; Annexure A | Official online/offline nomination channels; request acknowledgement; check a statement or confirmation of registration. Effective 1 September 2026. |
| HDFC Bank deposit accounts only | [HDFC Bank NetBanking FAQ](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/need-help/net-banking-faqs), “Where can I view my Nominee and Account Holder details?” and “Can I add or change nominee in my account online?” | Accounts → select account → Account Details → nominee/account-holder details. Sole holders may add/change there; bank recommends joint holders visit a branch with the other holders. Rechecked 3 October 2026. |
| Bank deposits | [Banking Companies (Nomination) Rules, 2025](https://thc.nic.in/Central%20Governmental%20Rules/Banking%20Companies%20(Nomination)%20Rules,%202025.pdf), rules 1(2), 2(2), 2(12)–(13), form acknowledgement section | Ask the bank for its current nomination form or available e-nomination. Distinguish receipt from recorded registration. Effective 1 November 2025. |
| MF service channel and demat units | [AMFI Investor Corner — Nomination](https://www.amfiindia.com/investor/become-mf-distributor?zoneName=nomination), “How to Nominate?” and “For Units held in Electronic (Demat) Mode” | Contact the fund/registrar; units in demat use that demat nomination route. The unusual URL was opened and its actual investor nomination content inspected. |

The newer SEBI circular supersedes earlier securities nomination circulars. AMFI's page/form may contain older operational details, so it is used only for the service-channel distinction, not a universal current signature/document checklist. Bank rules are a separate authority.

Virasat does not display one-nominee limits, automatic freeze deadlines, registration guarantees or a universal inheritance rule. Joint/minor/other special cases and actual forms are confirmed with the institution. “Not added” can reflect a deliberate opt-out; the account holder chooses the next action.

The institution's acknowledgement of receipt and the actual registration record are different observations. The tracker requires the user to say a reviewed record shows registration before using “Checked in a record.” No authenticity check is performed.

## Current provider routing

Canonical local institution IDs separate HDFC Bank, HDFC Securities and HDFC Mutual Fund. Search accepts names, abbreviations, selected regional aliases and custom institutions; it never sends the query to a provider. Canonical IDs are validated against both account type and institution name when importing saved data.

- HDFC savings: Accounts → account → Account Details → Nominee and Account Holder details. Deposit: FD/RD → choose the deposit → Add/Change Nominee. Joint-holder action uses the branch route. Each deposit is checked separately from savings. Source: [HDFC NetBanking FAQs](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/need-help/net-banking-faqs).
- Zerodha first addition: Console → Account → Nominees; its documentation describes offline handling for joint accounts and when Aadhaar-linked mobile access is unavailable. Coin holdings use the same demat nomination. Source: [Zerodha nomination help](https://support.zerodha.com/category/your-zerodha-account/nomination-process/articles/add-nominee-online-zerodha).
- HDFC MF folio: provider nomination service page and current form. All holdings under the relevant folio need the correct holder context; do not transfer a savings-account nomination onto a fund. Source: [HDFC Mutual Fund nomination](https://www.hdfcfund.com/services/registration-of-nominee).
- Zerodha correction/additional nominee: the current public help describes current nomination and account-modification forms, hand signatures, eSign and a support ticket. This is a separate route from the first-addition screen. Fees/timelines are left with the provider rather than promised in Virasat. Source: [Zerodha change instructions](https://support.zerodha.com/category/your-zerodha-account/nomination-process/articles/add-modify-or-remove-nominee).
- SBI deposits: published channels are YONO, Internet Banking and branches. The app gives channel-level guidance, not a claimed tested login sequence. Sources: [current nomination facility](https://sbi.bank.in/web/personal-banking/nomination-facility) and the nomination notice in [SBI Annual Report FY2025](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/17826/35696/Annual%2BReport%2BFY2025.pdf). The older NRI FAQ was inspected but its screen sequence and older limitations were not generalized to all accounts.
- Axis savings only: Services → Account Services → Update Nominee; choose account and add/change. The published workflow includes generating, completing, signing and uploading a form, then NetSecure verification. A branch is the alternative. FD/RD remains a general fallback. Source: [Axis savings nomination help](https://application.axis.bank.in/webforms/axis-support/sub-issues/sub-issues/Bank-SB-update-11.aspx).
- Groww demat: Profile → Account Details → Nominee Details; Edit Nominee for eligible investments. Existing/external fund folios may need a separate servicing route; the app does not treat a profile change as proof every folio changed. Source: [Groww nominee changes](https://groww.in/help/stocks%2C-f%26o%2C-ipo-%26-mtf/searchable/how-can-i-change-my-nominee-on-groww--62).
- Other institutions: general official rules and a question to ask their service team. No exact screen path is invented. CAMS' [nomination service overview](https://www.camsonline.com/Investors/Service-requests/Nomination/Nomination_Opt-in_or_Opt-out) was reviewed as a service-channel reference; it is not treated as universal fund coverage.

The SEBI circular permits a status indication rather than disclosure of nominee names in relevant statements. The app therefore separates “registration shown” from “nominee details checked.” The banking and securities regimes have different nominee limits; optional notes are bounded separately (four bank / three securities) and never presented as an allocation form or legal entitlement ruling.

A mutual-fund record linked to demat requires an explicit user check that the units and account holders match the exact demat statement. This is a user assertion, not independent verification. The family sheet identifies the linkage and does not duplicate nomination completion.

The route registry in `app/dist/guides.js` keeps the review date, applicability and source URL beside each route. General bank/broker/AMC assisted steps are a preparation aid, not a claim that every service centre accepts every request. Users are told to confirm the correct centre, current form, signatures and documents before travelling. Public help pages were reviewed; no logged-in institutional workflow was tested.
