# 04 — Cases and human evidence

Public posts are leads, not verified case files. No author was contacted, no private document pack was obtained, and no loss or recovery was independently audited. Names and account identifiers are deliberately unnecessary here.

## C1 — Grandmother's shares arrive; the history does not

**Source:** Reddit, 8 June 2024. [S030](sources.md#s030)

The writer says their grandmother closed an ICICI account and transferred shares to Zerodha. The receiving account displayed N/A for purchase price and requested manual purchase details. The shares had reportedly been bought during 2010–2012; the family did not remember the prices and the original account was closed.

**What it establishes:** a concrete user-reported missing-history problem following a successful closure-and-transfer.

**What it does not establish:** portfolio value, actual tax overpayment, impossibility of retrieving archived records, or the final outcome.

**Research use:** strongest opening story for the acquisition-history branch. Request anonymised old trade records and new credit statements only if the person independently consents in a later study.

## C2 — “I can enter the price, but where do I find it?”

**Source:** Reddit, 14 May 2025. [S031](sources.md#s031)

A user reports that their mother moved shares from Sharekhan to Zerodha. The shares appeared, but invested amount and P&L did not. They say the previous broker could not help them obtain the missing information.

**What it establishes:** the interface's manual-entry option may leave the hard information problem with the family.

**Limit:** the statement about broker assistance is an unverified report, not a finding against the broker. No amount or duration is established.

## C3 — A routine newer transfer as a control case

**Source:** Reddit, April 2025. [S032](sources.md#s032)

A Groww-to-Zerodha transfer discussion concerns missing average price; commenters suggest using the old tradebook. This may be resolvable through ordinary exports.

**Research use:** essential counterexample. A product should not label every transfer a crisis. Compare the effort of retrieving and importing a complete tradebook before claiming novel value.

## C4 — An issuer corrects its own calculation

**Source:** ITC's revised exchange filing. [S022](sources.md#s022)

The correction described in Chapter 03 is primary evidence that document versioning matters even when the source is authoritative.

**Research use:** a deterministic benchmark for detecting an inconsistent illustration and selecting the revised notice. This is a data-quality case, not a documented investor-loss case. There is no basis to multiply the illustration's difference by all ITC shareholders.

## C5 — A buyer encounters an obsolete seller identity

**Source:** NCLT Mumbai, Nauman Zahid vs Allied Digital Services, CP 137(MB)/2024; order 11 February 2026, hosted by SEBI. [S025](sources.md#s025)

The matter concerns 14,90,000 physical shares and a seller whose corporate identity changed before liquidation. The 2026 special window was brought to the tribunal's attention and the petition was disposed of at the petitioner's request.

**Limit:** the order did not establish that the claim was eligible under the window or that the investor recovered the shares. It is not a small-retail archetype and no rupee valuation is used.

**Research use:** ownership evidence can need both a security history and a counterparty history. Corporate genealogy alone is insufficient.

## C6 — Two deaths, one unchanged physical folio

**Source:** Reddit, 19 June 2026. [S066](sources.md#s066)

A poster describes a few thousand physical ITC shares jointly held by their parents. Their mother, the first holder, died intestate in 2004; the folio was not updated. The father later made a will and subsequently died. The claimant reports requests for documents relating to the mother's estate even though the father's will covered securities.

**What it establishes:** a compelling sequence-of-events problem in a public account. A current certificate can conceal several unresolved historical transitions.

**Limit:** no opinion here that the RTA's request was wrong, that the father's will alone settles ownership, or that the claim qualifies for a particular current route. The July 2026 framework postdates the post. Holdings and ₹ value are not independently verified.

**Research use:** strongest inheritance case for a history graph; too legally ambiguous to use as an automatically “solved” demo without expert-reviewed facts.

## C7 — Family encounters the deceased person's live-KYC prompt

**Source:** Reddit, 15 February 2026. [S067](sources.md#s067)

The writer says their father died in October 2025. Mutual-fund transmission was easier, but share accounts had different nomination situations. Attempting to sell through one account led to a live-photo/KYC request for the father; the poster also reports being told transmission was unavailable elsewhere.

**Research use:** shows the practical danger of taking the ordinary account-use route when a formal transmission route is needed. A useful system must route the claimant correctly rather than encourage use of the deceased person's login.

**Limit:** no independent confirmation of institutional responses, claim eligibility or monetary value. The report predates later process reforms.

## C8 — Investors manually reconstruct pre-2018 histories

**Source:** Reddit, July 2025. [S033](sources.md#s033)

A user describes compiling historic fair-market-value information with later corporate actions. This supports investigation of manual reconciliation effort, but neither the table nor its tax treatment was independently validated.

**Research use:** benchmark candidate after obtaining source documents and professional checking; do not import forum spreadsheets as authoritative price/tax data.

## C9 — One issuer page contains several generations of procedure

**Source:** KEC International's shareholder-advice page, inspected 1 October 2026. [S073](sources.md#s073)

Its transmission section reflects the July 2026 thresholds, while lower sections retain the 2025 physical-transfer window and a 2022 LOC-based service description. Compare those passages with the later January 2026 circulars in Chapter 03.

**Research use:** a concrete source-versioning fixture. Classifying an entire domain or page as “official and current” is insufficient: applicability must be checked at the individual provision level.

**Limit:** this observation does not establish that the RTA followed an obsolete process, rejected a valid claim, or caused a financial loss.

## Public voices: discovery, not validation

Nithin Kamath publicly explained in 2023 why user-entered acquisition values for transferred holdings could not be independently verified for the then verified-P&L feature. A June 2026 post discusses consolidation and manual acquisition-price updates. These support the existence of a trust boundary, not a measured market-loss estimate. [S035](sources.md#s035), [S036](sources.md#s036)

The Nikhil Kamath/Nandan Nilekani episode provides contextual discussion of fragmented financial information and Account Aggregator. Think School's Zerodha case discussion is broad market context. Neither establishes a novel acquisition-history solution or quantifies this problem. Only indexed transcript/summary material was inspected; these are not claims of watching the full videos. ## What is still missing

One consenting claimant with documents, a dated before/after record, and an independent reviewer. That would be stronger than ten additional anonymous posts. Until then, use a clearly labelled synthetic family scenario informed by these cases, never a fabricated “real customer saved ₹X” story.
